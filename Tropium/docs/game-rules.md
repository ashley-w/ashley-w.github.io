# TropeOut Game Rules

## Objective
The objective of the game is to solve a daily puzzle by using the provided tropes to create a story or scenario. Players must use their creativity and understanding of the tropes to craft a compelling narrative.

## Game Setup
1. Each day, a new set of tropes will be made available to players.
2. Players will have access to a limited time frame to complete the puzzle.

## How to Play
1. **Select Tropes**: At the start of each day, players will be presented with a selection of tropes.
2. **Create Your Story**: Using the selected tropes, players must write a short story or scenario.
3. **Submit Your Entry**: Once the story is complete, players can submit their entries for validation.

## Scoring
- Players will receive points based on creativity, adherence to the tropes, and overall narrative quality.
- Feedback will be provided after submission to help players improve.

## Rules
- Each story must incorporate all selected tropes.
- Stories should be original and not plagiarized.
- Players must submit their entries before the deadline to be considered for scoring.

## Tips for Success
- Familiarize yourself with the tropes and their meanings.
- Think outside the box and be creative in your storytelling.
- Review feedback from previous submissions to enhance your skills.

## Conclusion
Enjoy the challenge of crafting stories and have fun exploring the world of tropes!
